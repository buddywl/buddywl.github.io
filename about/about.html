Creating practical art has always been a goal of mine. As an engineer and computer science major, I am always looking for ways to make like a little bit easier, so this project was sort of a way for me to solve one of my most consistent problems: being late to class in the morning because I couldn't figure out what to wear. I hope to one day launch this website and make it a user to user experience where you can upload your own clothes to make it more practical for everyone to use. Hope you enjoyed playing with my closet!
